Clinic history:Established in 1889 by Dr. William Worrall Mayo and his sons, this clinic pioneered the multi-specialty group practice model to ensure the needs of the patient always come first. From its origins in a small Minnesota practice, it evolved into a global leader in medical education and research, achieving historic breakthroughs like the discovery of cortisone. Today, it operates as a world-renowned non-profit healthcare system, serving over 1.3 million patients annually across international campuses.

Mission:To provide patient-centered healthcare with excellence in quality, service, and access through compassionate clinical practice and innovative research.

Vision: To be the most trusted partner for healthcare, creating a community where all people achieve their full potential for lifelong well-being.

Services offered:Primary & Preventive Care: Comprehensive health screenings, routine check-ups, and 2026-standard vaccinations for all ages. Specialty Consultations: Expert care in Cardiology, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Orthopedics, and Mental Health. Diagnostics & Lab Services: On-site blood tests, ECGs, and advanced digital imaging with real-time report access. Chronic Disease Management: Personalized treatment plans and remote monitoring for hypertension, diabetes, and long-term wellness. Modern Healthcare Solutions: Integrated telemedicine for video consultations and convenient home sample collection services.

Doctors introduction:Our board-certified specialists combine decades of clinical expertise with compassionate, patient-centered care to deliver the highest standards of modern medical excellence.
